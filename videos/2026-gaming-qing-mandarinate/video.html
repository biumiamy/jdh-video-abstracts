How to play
1
Start · Your first throw decides your background,
from official’s son to medicine student.
2
Move · Each throw moves you from office to office,
up or down the ranks.
3
Goal · Retire at the highest rank you can reach,
and collect the most chips.
4
Shortcuts · Lucky throws let you jump ahead,
or you pay to buy an office.
